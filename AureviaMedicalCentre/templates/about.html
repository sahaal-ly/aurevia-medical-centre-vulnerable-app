Our story

More than two decades of healing

Founded in 1998, Aurevia Medical Centre began as a 30-bed community clinic. Today we are a multi-speciality hospital serving thousands of families every year with advanced diagnostics, expert clinicians and compassionate nursing.

Our mission is simple: deliver safe, ethical and affordable healthcare to everyone who walks through our doors.
